# Maktaba Notices

## OpenITI

Maktaba imports metadata and downloads book texts from the
[OpenITI `v2025.1.9` release](https://github.com/OpenITI/RELEASE/tree/v2025.1.9).

OpenITI data remains subject to the upstream project's license, attribution,
and distribution requirements. Review the upstream repository before publishing
the application or redistributing downloaded texts.

## Application Dependencies

Maktaba is built with Android SDK, Jetpack Compose, Kotlin, Kotlin Coroutines,
and OkHttp. Their respective licenses are provided by their upstream projects
and packaged dependency metadata.
